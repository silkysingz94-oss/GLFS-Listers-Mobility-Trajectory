Methodology

Data: Compound, household and EA boundary data are loaded from the configured GitHub repository.

GPS: Compound x is interpreted as longitude and y as latitude.

Movement: Records are sorted by CreationDate and grouped by enumerator and visit date. The marker is interpolated between recorded compound locations.

Household linkage: Household ParentGlobalID is matched to compound GlobalID.

Limitation: Connecting lines are straight-line representations between recorded GPS points, not continuous GPS tracks or measured road routes.
