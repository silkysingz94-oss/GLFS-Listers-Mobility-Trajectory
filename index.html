Methodology

Compound data: GPS records are read from the compound CSV. x is longitude and y is latitude.

Movement sequence: Records are ordered by CreationDate and grouped by enumerator and date.

Animation: The moving marker is interpolated between successive recorded GPS observations. The orange trail is progressively drawn as the animation advances.

Household linkage: Household ParentGlobalID is matched with compound GlobalID.

Limitation: The animation does not represent continuous GPS tracking, measured travel speed, or actual road routing.
