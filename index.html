Methodology

Routing source: OpenStreetMap-based pedestrian routing service using an OSRM-compatible route API.

Route calculation: Consecutive compound GPS coordinates are sent to the pedestrian-routing service. The returned route geometry is used for map display and animation.

Animation: The moving marker advances along the returned OSM route geometry, not a straight line between recorded compound coordinates.

Travel-time estimate: Returned route distance is divided by the selected assumed walking speed. This is an indicative estimate, not measured travel time.

Unrouted journeys: If the routing service cannot find a route, or a request fails, that leg is marked as unrouted. No straight-line route is substituted.

Household linkage: Household ParentGlobalID is matched with compound GlobalID.

Data attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Routing service availability and coverage are subject to the provider's terms and limits.
