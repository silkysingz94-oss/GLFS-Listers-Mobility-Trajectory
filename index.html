Methodology and limitations

Data: Compound CSV and EA JSON are fetched from the public GitHub repository. Household CSV is an optional linked dataset.

GPS: x is interpreted as longitude and y as latitude.

Movement: Records are sorted by CreationDate and grouped by enumerator and visit date.

Household linkage: Household ParentGlobalID is matched to compound GlobalID.

Limitation: Connecting lines are straight-line representations between recorded GPS points, not continuous GPS tracking or measured road routes.
